# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People exploring open-source AI agent projects, plus contributors and maintainers who help improve directory records.

## Product Purpose

AgentNine helps people discover open-source AI agent projects and review their source, setup, and documented access before running them. Success means visitors can make informed choices and the community can improve the project information.

## Positioning

Listings connect upstream repositories with practical setup notes, access details, and verification context.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The public directory supports browsing, search, comparison, and community submissions. Platform feedback may be sent without public-display permission, but is shown publicly only when the submitter explicitly permits display and an administrator approves it.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the AgentNine name and extend the existing website visual system. The owner explicitly requested that new feedback surfaces follow the current AgentNine theme.

## Evidence on Hand

The existing directory and its source, setup, and access information are real project content. Do not invent feedback, names, or testimonials. Only approved, consented submissions are eligible for the public homepage.

## Product Principles

- Make upstream sources and relevant setup context easy to inspect.
- Distinguish documented information from endorsements or guarantees.
- Keep community submissions subject to review before they change public content.
- Do not present fabricated feedback as real.
